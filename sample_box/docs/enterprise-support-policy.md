# Enterprise Support Policy

This document is restricted to support leads.

## Escalation

Enterprise incidents with customer impact should be escalated to the support lead within 30 minutes.

If a customer has more than three open priority cases or an unresolved case older than 14 days, the account should be marked as elevated risk for the weekly support review.

## Response Targets

Priority 1 cases require an initial response within 15 minutes.

Priority 2 cases require an initial response within 2 hours.

Priority 3 cases require an initial response within 1 business day.
